Question
How much import concentration reflects dominant source countries within products?
Supports yes if
Importer-product sourcing is dominated by one supplier and source HHI remains high in country-year summaries.
Weakens yes if
Supplier shares are diffuse within products or only globally concentrated in H2.4 rather than importer-specific.
Current result
In 2024, median weighted top-supplier share is 51.6% and median weighted source HHI is 0.388.
