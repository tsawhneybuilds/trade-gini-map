Question
Do high-concentration export states predict meaningfully different later export-growth paths?
Supports yes if
High-concentration buckets show systematically different later trade or active-product growth across metrics.
Weakens yes if
Bucket differences are small, unstable across metrics, or mostly sample-composition artifacts.
Current result
Across published buckets and horizons, the reported mean ranges from 5.1% to 7.8%; this remains descriptive.

Trade growth

How does later trade growth vary across concentration buckets?

Question this answers
Do later trade-growth paths differ across base-raw HHI buckets?
Supports the reading if
Mean annualized growth differs systematically across ordered concentration buckets.
Weakens the reading if
Bucket means are similar, unordered, or based on very different samples.
Current result
Across published buckets and horizons, the reported mean ranges from 5.1% to 7.8%; this remains descriptive.

Active-product growth

Do concentrated states add products more slowly or more quickly?

Question this answers
Do base-raw HHI buckets predict different active-product growth?
Supports the reading if
Active-product growth changes systematically across the concentration buckets.
Weakens the reading if
Product-count growth is flat or unstable across buckets.
Current result
Across published buckets and horizons, the reported mean ranges from -0.0% to 3.2%; this remains descriptive.
